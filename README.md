# 🛡️ Sentinel Client

*Sentinel Client* is an all-in-one **Gorilla Tag mod checker and utility client** designed for Meta Quest. Featuring automatic player checking, live nametags, one-press reporting, lobby hopping, friends and party features, and a fully customisable wrist disc.

## 📱 About

Sentinel Client brings useful tools and player information directly into Gorilla Tag through an easy-to-access wrist disc.

Whether you're checking players for modifications, finding friends, switching lobbies or customising your experience, Sentinel keeps everything in one place.

## ✨ Features

### 🔍 Mod Checker

Every player in the room is automatically checked when they join.

Open **Players** on the disc to see who's:
- 🟢 Clean
- 🟠 Modded
- 🔴 Flagged

### 🚨 One-Press Report

When a player is flagged, reporting them is just one press on the disc.

No digging through menus or manually typing player names.

### 🏷️ Live Nametags

See useful information displayed above players, including:
- Player name
- Refresh rate
- Platform

Customise what appears under **Settings → Tags**.

### 🌍 Lobby Hop

Not enjoying your current lobby?

Hop into a fresh lobby directly from the wrist disc without needing to take off your headset or use a PC.

### 👥 Friends & Parties

- Add other players.
- See which friends are online.
- Check where your friends are playing.
- Join their lobbies with your party.

### 🎨 Make It Yours

Customise Sentinel Client to suit your preferences.

- Recolour the menu.
- Set your own keybinds.
- Adjust wind effects.
- Personalise your client experience.

Try changing **Menu Colour** to see the interface change!

## ⌚ Lives on Your Wrist

Sentinel Client lives directly on your wrist disc.

Look down and it's there. Look up and it's gone.

No second screen. No unnecessary distractions. Just the tools you need, right inside Gorilla Tag.

## 📥 Installation

1. Download the latest Sentinel Client release.
2. Extract the downloaded `.zip` file.
3. Follow the included installation instructions.
4. Install the required mod files.
5. Launch Gorilla Tag and enjoy Sentinel Client!

## ⭐ Connect Spotify

You get a Spotify Client ID by creating an app in the [Spotify Developer Dashboard](https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard).


1. Sign in with your Spotify account and create an app.

2. Add this redirect URI exactly: http://127.0.0.1:27381/callback

3.  Open the app’s settings and copy its Client ID.

4. Put the Client ID in config.txt on your VR. Android/Data/nkhhi.gorillatag/config.txt
